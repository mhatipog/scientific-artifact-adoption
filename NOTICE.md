# Research provenance and licensing

This is an independent scientific reproduction attempt, not an official fork or endorsement.

Original research: M. Rußwurm, C. Pelletier, M. Zollner, S. Lefèvre and M. Körner, *BreizhCrops: A Time Series Dataset for Crop Type Mapping* (ISPRS Archives, 2020). DOI: https://doi.org/10.5194/isprs-archives-XLIII-B2-2020-1545-2020.

Original source: https://github.com/dl4sits/BreizhCrops, pinned to commit 6de796ed36a457c8520322d6110b8f2862fd8c25. This workflow downloads the original project; it does not modify or relicense its model or preprocessing. No original dataset or pretrained weights are stored in this project.

**License caveat:** Upstream LICENSE says GPL-3.0 but upstream setup.py metadata says MIT. The copyright holders must clarify this inconsistency before any relicensing. Assets may have separate rights.

The original Transformer has passed only an independent modern-PyTorch synthetic compatibility test. Actual Belle-Île accuracy reproduction has **not** been established yet.

Independent research on thin-strip contact-line dynamics and contact-angle hysteresis is tracked separately and is not endorsed by the BreizhCrops researchers.
