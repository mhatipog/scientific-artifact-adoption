# Round 16 — Spectrally indistinguishable, nonlinearly opposite capillary onsets

**18 October 2026.** Theoretical extension of the *already established* Round-8 phase-obstruction theorem and Round-14 5/7 design. **No new experiment and no claim of non-neutral 3D stability.** Code: [spectral_isolation.py](spectral_isolation.py); deterministic [tests](tests/test_spectral_isolation.py), [derived CSV](derived/neutral_plate_jacobi_spectrum.csv), [figure](figures/jacobi_spectral_isolation.svg).

## Decision and scientific result

The useful new assertion is a **finite-spectral indistinguishability theorem**: in the reduced smooth-wall Young–Laplace class, for every finite roughness-correlation order, any prescribed finite number of continuum *linear* capillary Jacobi eigenvalues can become arbitrarily close for a pair of channels whose *nonlinear* fixed-volume two-front pitchfork quartics retain opposite nonzero signs. It is conditional on regular, physically unoccluded critical cylindrical surfaces, ideal Young wetting and neutral plates.

This is a stronger *in-house extension* of [Round 8](../ROUND8_ARBITRARY_ORDER_THEOREM.md), **not** a newly discovered symmetry-breaking mechanism. It also does **not** make the *entire unbounded spectrum* uniformly close: for high eigenvalue index, tiny arclength changes can produce large absolute eigenvalue differences.

### Exact neutral-plate reduction and the overlooked Robin curvature term

Let the sidewalls be y=±w(x)/2, with actual sidewall Young angle θ, plate contact angle 90°, and finite depth d. A circular meniscus at wall intersection x=s has

- wall inclination α=arctan(w'(s)/2), apparent angle θ_a=θ+α;
- circle curvature κ=p(s)=2cos θ_a/w(s), half-arclength L=(π/2−θ_a)/κ;
- signed sidewall curvature κ_w=w''(s)/[2(1+(w'(s)/2)^2)^(3/2)].

The **physical linearized freely moving contact-line Robin condition** at either wall, using outward arclength normal, is

\[
\partial_\nu u+\beta_{\rm wall}u=0,\qquad
\beta_{\rm wall}=\kappa\cot\theta+\frac{\kappa_w}{\sin\theta}.
\]

The sign convention for κ_w corresponds to the upper wall's graph y=w/2; reversing the signed support curvature convention reverses the displayed sign (cf. [Bostwick–Steen 2015](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-fluid-010814-013626) and [Finn 1984](https://www.numdam.org/article/AST_1984__118__157_0.pdf), both known second-variation/contact-line theory). **Omitting the support-curvature term is not acceptable.**

At a pressure-stationary critical state p'(s_c)=0, direct differentiation of the Young–Laplace pressure gives the exact identity

\[
\beta_{\rm wall}=\kappa\cot\theta_a
                 =\kappa\tan\phi,\qquad
\phi:=\kappa L=\frac\pi2-\theta_a.
\]

This identity holds **at the stationary state only**. In particular, using β=κtanφ at all other positions would manufacture a false zero Jacobi mode along the entire equilibrium branch. The code evaluates the two expressions independently, including w'', and checks agreement below 2×10^-9 mm^-1 at the selected roots. At ±1 μm away, they diverge by roughly 0.50–1.39 mm^-1.

The neutral-plate cylinder has exact Jacobi operator and natural boundaries

\[
\mathcal J=-\partial_q^2-\partial_z^2-\kappa^2,\quad
q\in(-L,L),\ z\in(0,d),\quad
u_z(q,0)=u_z(q,d)=0.
\]

The critical mode generated by differentiating the *exact* circular family is
\(\psi(q)=A\cos(\kappa q)\), with
\(A=1+ww''/[4(1+(w'/2)^2)]>0\). It satisfies the physical Robin condition and is nodeless; hence it is the lowest planar Sturm–Liouville eigenfunction. This spectral separation was established qualitatively in **our prior Round 8**; the present audit adds independent support-curvature enforcement, quantitative spectral gaps and FEM convergence.

Even transverse roots t obey \(t\tan t=\beta_{\rm wall} L\). Odd roots obey \(-t\cot t=\beta_{\rm wall}L\). The first even root is t=φ and gives λ_0=0. The first odd root \(t_o\in(\pi/2,\pi)\) gives the first positive planar eigenvalue. At neutral plates the **full continuum** eigenvalues (not a finite polynomial ansatz) are

\[
\lambda_{j,n}=(t_j/L)^2-\kappa^2+(n\pi/d)^2,\quad n\ge0.
\]

Consequently the exact local noncritical spectral gap is

\[
\boxed{\Delta_{\rm 3D}=
\min\left\{\frac{t_o^2-\phi^2}{L^2},
\frac{\pi^2}{d^2}\right\}.}
\]

This is the unconstrained single-cap Jacobi spectrum; in the ideal two-cap *fixed-volume* problem, the global volume constraint removes the symmetric critical combination and retains the antisymmetric transfer mode. Its use assumes no additional interactions, bulk modes or earlier global instabilities. This calculation does not certify a globally stable branch or a non-neutral contact line.

## Strengthened theorem and proof

For any finite integer N, use the Round-8 geometries, with odd M>2N:

\[
w_{\pm,M}(x)=1+0.05\cos(kx)\ \pm\frac{0.046875}{M^4}
[\cos(Mkx)-\cos((M+2)kx)]\ \mathrm{mm},
\quad k=\frac{2\pi}{0.3\ \mathrm{mm}}.
\]

Take the previously computed simple local quartic-degeneracy base point
\(\theta_* = 37.1451464361036^\circ\),
\(s_0=0.698614069163614\) mm,
\(p_0'(s_0)=0,\ p_0''(s_0)\ne0,\ B_{4,0}(s_0)=0\).
These base values are **numerical** and would require interval verification to make the following parameter choices an unconditional computer-assisted theorem.

**Proposition (conditional on the base point and regularity):** Along an infinite subsequence of odd M, the two geometries have identical *all* translation-invariant roughness n-point correlations for n≤N (Round-8 exact combinatorial argument), their local 3D neutral-plate Jacobi eigenvalues satisfy

\[
|\lambda_{j,n}^{(+)}(M)-\lambda_{j,n}^{(-)}(M)|=O_{j,n}(M^{-2})
\]

for every *fixed* pair of indices (j,n), while
\(B_{4,+}(M) B_{4,-}(M)<0\) and
\(\liminf |B_{4,\pm}(M)|>0\) on that subsequence (subject to positive physical interface clearance).

**Proof.** For the signed Fourier perturbation b=O(M^-4), the perturbations of \(w,w',w'',w''',w''''\) are respectively O(M^-4), O(M^-3), O(M^-2), O(M^-1), O(1). Analytic p'(s) depends through w'', hence the implicit-function theorem at simple p''(s_0) gives \(s_{\pm,M}-s_0=O(M^{-2})\). It follows that κ, θ_a, L and the **full physical Robin coefficient**, which includes w'', are each O(M^-2) from their common base values (at the root β_wall=κcot θ_a). The self-adjoint Robin Sturm–Liouville eigenvalues vary smoothly in these coefficients for any fixed index. Adding the *identical* depth Neumann spectrum yields the O(M^-2) pairwise eigenvalue difference.

Meanwhile the quartic depends on \(p'''\) and therefore w''''. The **already established** Round-8 expansion is

\[
B_{4,\pm}(M)
=\pm0.046875\,\mathcal C
[\cos(Mu_0)-\cos((M+2)u_0)] + O(M^{-1}),
\]

with \(\mathcal C=8793.28959470\), \(u_0=ks_0\), in that report's units. There is an infinite odd-M subsequence on which the oscillatory factor is bounded away from zero. Thus both leading terms have nonvanishing opposite signs. The Fourier resonance counting in Round 8 guarantees n-point correlation equality for every n≤N. QED *conditional on the quoted base degeneracy and physical regularity*.

**Not claimed:** uniform closeness over *all* eigenvalues, eigenfunction closeness in arbitrary norms, non-neutral plate-angle continuation, experimental detectability or priority over all published mathematical literature.

- [Wells et al. (2018)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-018-03840-6) and [Bisquert et al. (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1021/acs.langmuir.4c02908): smooth-topography capillary snaps and competing fold/pitchfork mechanisms **already known**.

- [Bostwick and Steen (2015)](https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-fluid-010814-013626) and [Finn (1984)](https://www.numdam.org/article/AST_1984__118__157_0.pdf): contact-line second variations and wall curvature effects **already known**.

The particular *joint non-identifiability* of finite roughness statistics **and a finite capillary linear stability spectrum** for nonlinear pitchfork class may be a publishable synthesis, but this literature search is insufficient to establish historical priority. Equations are a conditional model theorem, not experimental observations.

**Strongest reviewer objection:** a mathematically tunable O(M^-4) roughness perturbation ceases to be physical at the harmonics used to prove arbitrary spectral indistinguishability; the finite 5/7 pair is only locally robust in a reduced model with unmeasured Young angle, and no valid non-neutral 3D bifurcation-class validation or fabricated independent onset comparison exists.

**Highest-value next gate:** independently converge the constrained nonlinear 3D free-contact-line quartic and Hessian at **90° first**, benchmark it against the exact \(d B_4^{2D}\) and the present analytic eigenvalues, then continue **89° and 88°** with real sidewall angle/topography. In parallel, accurately measure the actual chip profile if fabrication is attempted.
